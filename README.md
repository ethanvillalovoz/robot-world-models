# Robot World Models

Research exploration with Bo Zhu.

## Direction

Environment/world modeling for robotics.

The goal is to investigate a component of a larger robotics research
picture: how a robot represents and reasons about the physical environment
in order to predict the consequences of its actions.

## Constraints

- No dependency on collecting new physical-robot experiments
- Core experiments should be possible in simulation
- Robotics motivates the problem
- Main technical focus is environment/world modeling
- Potential target: ICML

## Current Status

We have not defined the research question yet.

The immediate goal is to understand the recent literature and identify
2–3 promising research questions to discuss with Bo.
